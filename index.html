    Hospital Choice Dashboard

    Compares acute care hospitals with the national rate on safety, mortality, and readmissions, so a reader can see how a hospital performs without knowing the measures.

    • 952acute care hospitals
    • 5states
    • 3quality measure groups

    Key takeaway: a reader can see whether a hospital is better than, the same as, or worse than the national rate on mortality, a category CMS does not publish.

    • Defines a mortality performance category where CMS provides none, with explicit rules for better, same, and worse than the national rate.
    • Reports results in plain language.
    • Shows hospitals with no result instead of dropping them.
