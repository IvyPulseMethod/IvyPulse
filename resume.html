The journey, stop by stop

  1. 2016–2018 · Calgary, AB

    Where it started: the sidelines

    My interest in health started on a high school track team, where I served as the team’s sports medicine trainer.

    Sports Medicine Trainer, Western Canada High School

    Feb 2016 – Jun 2018
    • Treated athletes’ minor injuries and administered pre-season baseline tests.
    • Worked with the head coach to teach athletes about nutrition and hydration.
    Added to the pack
    • First Aid (HCP)
    • Heads Up concussion training
    • Injury care
    • Baseline testing

  2. 2020–2024 · Kelowna, BC

    Learning the science, leading on campus

    I earned a Bachelor of Human Kinetics at the University of British Columbia and took on more responsibility outside the classroom along the way.

    Milestone

    Wellness Wave, Co-Creator & Program Lead · Sep 2023 – Apr 2024

    A physical activity and wellness program for older adults in residential communities, which I co-created and launched.

    1. 25participants at launch
    2. 4sites across Kelowna
    3. 30+paid volunteers and staff
    4. UBCadopted it as a practicum site

    Residence Advisor, University of British Columbia

    Sep 2020 – Apr 2021
    • Supported first-year students through academic, personal, and mental health challenges, with mentorship, referrals, and crisis response.

    Member, President’s Non-Academic Misconduct Committee, University of British Columbia

    Sep 2020 – Apr 2024
    • Reviewed evidence, assessed source credibility, and interviewed involved parties.
    • Contributed to formal written recommendations to the UBC President’s Office.

    Program Resource Advisor, University of British Columbia

    Sep 2021 – Apr 2022
    • Led a 25-member committee planning athletic and residence programming, including scheduling, budgeting, and marketing.

    Research Assistant, University of British Columbia

    Jan 2024 – May 2024
    • Ran structured literature reviews for a new Health and Exercise Sciences course on climate change and nutrition.
    • Summarized population-level evidence for research and program leads.
    Added to the pack
    • Literature review
    • Program design & evaluation
    • Evidence analysis
    • Formal reporting
    • Budgeting
    • Team leadership

  3. Nov 2024 – Aug 2025 · Calgary, AB

    On the front line of patient care

    Back in Calgary, I worked in two busy physiotherapy clinics, first on the administrative side and then as a kinesiologist.

    Patient Experience Coordinator, Interactive Health Physiotherapy

    Nov 2024 – Feb 2025
    • Managed complex WCB and MVC patient files, billing submissions, and claim timelines.
    • Kept accurate, confidential records under privacy legislation and WCB standards.

    Kinesiologist, Interactive Health Physiotherapy & Country Hills Physiotherapy

    Jan 2025 – Aug 2025
    • Managed a daily caseload of 10–15 patients, 85% of them WCB or MVC claimants.
    • Ran functional capacity assessments and wrote return-to-work recommendations.
    • Coordinated care across 5+ disciplines, from physiotherapy to family medicine.
    Added to the pack
    • Clinical documentation
    • Functional capacity assessment
    • Billing & compliance
    • Care coordination

  4. August 2025

    The fork in the trail

    I stepped away from clinical practice to study health data analytics full time.

  5. Sep 2025 – Present · Denton, TX

    You are here

    Building the data toolkit

    I’m completing a Master of Science in Health Data Analytics at the University of North Texas (December 2026) while working as a graduate assistant.

    Graduate Assistant, Executive & Professional Learning, University of North Texas

    Sep 2025 – Present
    • Collect, organize, and analyze enrollment data for program performance monitoring and reporting.
    • Build Power BI research dashboards and maintain a relational database for an internal research initiative.
    • Support the director in planning and promoting professional development programs.
    Added to the pack
    • Power BI
    • Tableau
    • SQL
    • Python
    • R
    • Excel
    • Qualtrics
