    Medicaid Expansion and the Black–White Preterm Birth Gap

    Describes how preterm birth rates differ by maternal race in states that expanded Medicaid and in states used for comparison.

    • 2016–2024years of birth data
    • 2expansion states
    • 7comparison states
    • Revised from a causal to a descriptive design after data checks showed the comparison groups were trending differently, so conclusions match the strength of the evidence.
    • The brief will state what the evidence shows, who could act, and what measure would show improvement.

About

Jennifer Gideon is a clinician turned health data analyst, completing a Master of Science in Health Data Analytics at the University of North Texas in December 2026. She holds a Bachelor of Human Kinetics from the University of British Columbia.

Before analytics she worked as a kinesiologist, providing direct patient care within an interdisciplinary team. That experience shapes how she reports data: for clinicians, leaders, and non-technical staff who need to act on it.
